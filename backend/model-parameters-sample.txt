Interpretation:
- Lower temperature is more repeatable and stays close to the documented facts; higher temperature permits more variation in wording.
- max_tokens=12 stops generation early with finish_reason=length, while the larger cap allows the complete answer. Output tokens are billed, so the cap controls cost.
- stop ends generation when the configured sequence appears. It is useful only when that boundary is reliable; a poorly chosen sequence can truncate a valid answer.

Recommended grounded-task settings:
- temperature: 0 to 0.2 for stable, factual answers.
- max_tokens: a task-sized cap, such as 100 for a short RAG answer, to prevent rambling and limit output cost.
- stop: optional, only for a known answer boundary. Tune top_p instead of temperature, not both together.
